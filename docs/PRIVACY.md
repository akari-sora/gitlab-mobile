# Mergeline privacy policy

Effective: 2026-10-30. Applies to the Mergeline app on Google Play (`app.mergeline`) and the GitLab-channel build (`io.gitlabmobile.gitlab_mobile`).

Mergeline is an independent client for GitLab®. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by GitLab Inc. The developer runs **no server, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking**. The app does not sell or rent data.

## What leaves your device, and where it goes

| What | Sent to | When |
|---|---|---|
| Your GitLab token or OAuth sign-in, and the requests you make (projects, issues, merge requests, files, CI, comments and other changes) | The GitLab instance you sign in to (GitLab.com or your own server), over HTTPS | While you use the app |
| Prompts, the page or files you ask about, read-only tool results, and attachment names and sizes (not their contents) | The AI provider **you** configure, with **your own** API key | Only after you add a provider and agree to the consent screen, and only when you send a message |
| Changes the AI proposes | Your GitLab instance | Only after you approve each one (or after you choose a more automatic level in settings) |
| An AI content report: the reason, your description, app version, the AI provider preset, API format and model, time, the answer's number in the conversation and, only if you choose to attach it, the reported AI answer (with secrets masked; it can still contain whatever the answer quoted from your project). You see the exact text before sending | The developer, through a Google Form (Google Forms' privacy policy applies to its handling) | Only when you report an AI answer |
| Images and media linked from Markdown | The site hosting them, without any GitLab credential | When a page shows them |
| Update checks | Google Play (Play build) or GitLab.com releases, anonymously (GitLab-channel build) | When the app checks for updates |

Each recipient (your GitLab instance, your AI provider, Google) handles data under its own terms. Mergeline cannot delete data that a recipient already holds.

## What stays on your device

Accounts and tokens, provider settings and keys, drafts and preferences are kept in the device's secure storage. Optional AI chat history (off by default), the AI write log, the read cache and offline repository copies are encrypted on the device. Demo mode uses built-in sample data and saves nothing.

## Your choices

- Use the app without AI: AI does nothing until you add a provider.
- Remove an account in Me: this deletes that account's token, drafts, caches, AI history and AI settings from the device. It does not delete your GitLab account or data on GitLab.
- Clear AI history, the read cache or offline copies in settings at any time.
- AI providers may keep logs under their own policies; check your provider's terms.

## Delete your data

Mergeline (developer: CB Studio) keeps no account database. To delete your data:

1. **On the device:** in the app, open Me, choose the account and remove it from this device. This deletes that account's token, drafts, caches, offline copies, AI history, AI write log and AI settings. Uninstalling the app deletes all of its local data.
2. **AI content reports you sent:** email aa22396584@gmail.com with the subject "Mergeline data deletion" and the approximate time of the report. The reports are deleted within 30 days; nothing is kept afterwards.
3. **Data on GitLab or at your AI provider** is held by them; use their deletion tools.

## Children

Mergeline is a developer tool and is not directed at children.

## Contact

aa22396584@gmail.com. Changes to this policy are published at this address with a new effective date.

# Mergeline 隱私權政策

生效日期：2026-10-30。適用於 Google Play 上的 Mergeline（`app.mergeline`）以及 GitLab 通道版本（`io.gitlabmobile.gitlab_mobile`）。

Mergeline 是 GitLab® 的獨立客戶端，與 GitLab Inc. 無關，也未經其認可。開發者**不經營任何伺服器，也沒有分析、廣告或追蹤**，不出售或出租任何資料。

## 哪些資料會離開你的裝置、送到哪裡

| 資料 | 送往 | 時機 |
|---|---|---|
| 你的 GitLab Token 或 OAuth 登入，以及你的操作（專案、議題、合併請求、檔案、CI、留言與其他修改） | 你登入的 GitLab（GitLab.com 或你自架的伺服器），使用 HTTPS | 使用 App 時 |
| 你的提問、你要 AI 看的頁面或檔案、唯讀工具的結果、附件的名稱與大小（不含內容） | **你自己**設定的 AI 供應商，使用**你自己**的 API Key | 只有在你新增供應商、同意確認畫面，並送出訊息後 |
| AI 提議的修改 | 你的 GitLab | 只有在你逐一核准後（或你在設定中選擇了更自動的等級） |
| AI 內容檢舉：原因、你的說明、App 版本、AI 供應商預設、API 格式與模型、時間、該回答在對話中的序號，以及（只有在你選擇附上時）被檢舉的 AI 回答（秘密已遮蔽，但仍可能含有回答引用的專案內容）。送出前你會看到完整內容 | 開發者，透過 Google 表單（Google 表單的處理適用 Google 的隱私權政策） | 只有在你檢舉 AI 回答時 |
| Markdown 中連結的圖片與媒體 | 存放它們的網站，不帶任何 GitLab 憑證 | 頁面顯示時 |
| 檢查更新 | Google Play（Play 版），或以匿名方式查詢 GitLab.com 的 release（GitLab 通道版） | App 檢查更新時 |

各接收方（你的 GitLab、你的 AI 供應商、Google）依其自身條款處理資料。對方已經持有的資料，Mergeline 無法替你刪除。

## 留在裝置上的資料

帳號與 Token、供應商設定與金鑰、草稿與偏好設定存放在裝置的安全儲存區。選用的 AI 聊天紀錄（預設關閉）、AI 寫入紀錄、讀取快取與離線儲存的儲存庫都在裝置上加密。Demo 模式使用內建的範例資料，不會儲存任何東西。

## 你的選擇

- 不用 AI 也能使用 App：沒有新增供應商之前，AI 不會做任何事。
- 在「我的」移除帳號：會從裝置刪除該帳號的 Token、草稿、快取、AI 紀錄與 AI 設定，但不會刪除你的 GitLab 帳號或 GitLab 上的資料。
- 隨時可以在設定中清除 AI 紀錄、讀取快取或離線副本。
- AI 供應商可能依其政策保留紀錄，請參考你所用供應商的條款。

## 刪除你的資料

Mergeline（開發者：CB Studio）沒有帳號資料庫。刪除資料的方式：

1. **裝置上的資料：** 在 App 的「我的」選擇帳號並從此裝置移除，會刪除該帳號的 Token、草稿、快取、離線副本、AI 紀錄、AI 寫入紀錄與 AI 設定。解除安裝 App 會刪除它所有的本機資料。
2. **你送出的 AI 內容檢舉：** 寄信到 aa22396584@gmail.com，主旨寫「Mergeline data deletion」並附上大約的檢舉時間。檢舉會在 30 天內刪除，之後不保留任何內容。
3. **GitLab 或 AI 供應商上的資料** 由他們保管，請使用他們的刪除工具。
